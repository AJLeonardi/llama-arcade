# Llama Baseball 3D

You are **Leonardo Benjamin** (#7). Start on the Pine Valley High Llamas, win the high school season and
the playoffs, get picked by the **Boston Strikers** for college, and then beat the **Providence Lightning**
(they've been to 7 Finals!) in the Finals, at night under the lights. It's built to look and play like
MLB The Show 26.

## Modes

- **⭐ Road to the Finals**: the career. High school season (win 2 of 3), high school playoffs (win both),
  college with the Boston Strikers (win 2 of 3), then the Finals in Providence (win it). Lose a playoff
  game and you play it again. It saves after every game.
- **🏟️ Pick Your Franchise**: pick any team, pick who they play, one game.
- **🏡 Backyard Baseball**: kids in the backyard, a wooden fence and the house behind home plate.
  Short fences, so home runs are easier.

Games are 3 innings. A half inning ends after 3 outs or 5 runs.

## How to play

**Batting** (you see it from behind the catcher):
- Drag anywhere (or arrow keys / WASD) to move the **hitting circle** over where the ball will cross.
- Tap **SWING** (or Space) when the ball gets there. Early pulls it to left field, late goes to right.
- Circle a bit **below** the ball = fly ball (home runs!). Circle **above** the ball = ground ball.
- Don't swing at balls outside the box: four balls is a walk.

**Pitching** (you see it from behind the pitcher):
- Pick a pitch: **FB** fastball, **CB** curveball (drops), **SL** slider (slides away), **CH** changeup
  (slow). Keys 1-4.
- Drag (or arrow keys) to move the yellow target.
- Tap **PITCH**, then tap **NOW!** when the shrinking ring turns green on the target. Late or early
  and the pitch misses its spot.

Fielding and running happen by themselves.

## For grown-ups

- Everything (the players, the parks, the crowd, the sounds) is drawn in code; no image files besides the icon.
- `?play=career`, `?play=backyard` or `?play=franchise` in the URL jumps straight into a game (for testing).
- The teams and players are made up. MLB The Show is the inspiration for the camera views, the hitting
  circle ("Big Zone") and the scorebug.
