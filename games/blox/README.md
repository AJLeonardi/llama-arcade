# 🦙 Llama-blox

A blocky 3D world, a bit like Roblox. Run around **Llama Island**, jump through the **obbies**
(obstacle courses), collect coins, and spend them on outfits in the **Dress-Up Shop**.

## How to play

- **Who are you?** In 👕 Dress Up, pick a blocky **llama** or a blocky **person**, then choose colours.
  Hats, glasses, capes, gold and rainbow cost coins.
- **On the island**, walk into a glowing doorway to start an obby:
  - 🌈 **Rainbow Run** (easy): stepping stones, lava stripes, a trampoline and a speed pad.
  - 🍭 **Candy Climb** (medium): stairs, a sliding platform, vanishing blocks, a conveyor belt and a lift.
  - 🏰 **Sky Castle** (hard): tiny stones, sliding lava, a giant speed jump and more.
- **Coins** float along the way. Reaching the 🏆 finish pad gives bonus coins and a ⭐ on the doorway.
- Walk into the pink-and-white shop stand (or tap 👕) to dress up.

### The worlds (the doorways on the west side of the island)

- 🔑 **Jail Break**: you've been locked in the Silly Jail for eating all the cookies! Find the red key in your
  cell, crawl through the air vents (you duck automatically), hop across the laundry on the washing machines,
  find the blue key, sneak past the 🔦 searchlights in the yard (if one finds you, you go back to the last
  checkpoint) and bounce over the wall on the mattress.
- 🚀 **Rocket Lab**: stand by the launch pad and tap **🔧 Build a rocket**. Stack parts from the bottom up
  (engine first, llama capsule on top), then **🚀 LAUNCH!** It's meant to be *very* hard: too heavy and it
  won't lift off, no fins and it flips over, and the Moon needs stages (✂️ splitters drop empty parts) and
  side boosters. The card after each flight says what to try next. Clouds, space, the Moon and the stars
  each give coins the first time; the Moon earns the ⭐.
- 🎢 **Coaster Park**: walk onto a ride's platform and tap **Ride!** Wiggly Worm (little), Loopy Llama (a
  loop), Twisty Tornado (a spiral), Sky Screamer (the big one) and the Big Wheel. **👀 My eyes** switches to
  riding view. The first ride on each gives coins; riding them all earns the ⭐.
- 🍔 **Llama Burgers**: walk to a station and its buttons appear. Cook a patty on the grill, make a burger
  (or cheeseburger) on the board, fry fries, pour drinks, scoop ice cream, then **🛎️ Serve** at the counter
  or at the 🚗 drive-through window. Bubbles show what each customer wants. Every 10 orders gives a bonus
  (and the ⭐). Customers wait a long time; if one gives up they just go home.

- 🐠 **Fish Tank to the Ocean** (the doorway on the east side): pick your fish (clownfish, goldfish, bass,
  blue tang, pufferfish or baby shark) and roll it in its fish bowl all the way from a pet store to the ocean in
  San Francisco, in 7 levels (play any of them): 1. Pet Store & Home (roll into the bag so someone buys you, then
  get out through the aquarium filter before the ⏰ 3-minute clock runs out, or start again in the house),
  2. Down the Pipes, 3. Stinky Sewer, 4. Rooftop Roll, 5. Power Line Panic, 6. Hilly Streets (Lombard Street and a
  cable car), 7. Golden Gate to the Ocean. The bowl rolls and keeps rolling (JUMP hops). A big fall, the green
  goo, hot pipes or sparks **crack** it and you go back to the last checkpoint. Tap **🐟 Flop out!** to jump out
  of the bowl: a fish jumps higher and fits through small gaps, but has only **50 seconds of air** (the bar at
  the top) until it lands in something full of water (a 🍼 water bottle rolls faster, a 🪣 bucket is tougher, a
  spare bowl). Every level cleared gives coins; clearing all 7 earns the ⭐. Designed by our resident 7-year-old.

| Block | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🟥 Orange lava | Touching it sends you back to the last checkpoint ("Whoops!"). Nobody gets hurt. |
| 🟦 Blue with a flag | Checkpoint: the flag turns green and you'll restart here. |
| 🩷 Pink rings | Trampoline: boing! |
| 🟨 Yellow arrows | Speed pad: run super fast for a moment. |
| 🟪 Purple | Vanishes soon after you land on it, so keep going! (It comes back.) |
| ⬛ Grey arrows | Conveyor belt: pushes you along. |
| 🏁 Checks | Finish! |

## For grown-ups

- Saves (coins, outfits, finished obbies and worlds, the rocket you built) are kept on the device, in the
  arcade's storage slot `blox`.
- All content is non-violent: falling, touching lava or getting spotted just puts you back at the last
  checkpoint. Rockets that fail fizzle or tumble, and the llama always parachutes home.
- Single player for now. Playing together on two devices could come later (see the arcade's
  `/new-game` notes on QR pairing).
- Everything (blocks, characters, sounds) is made in code: no image or sound files except the icon.
