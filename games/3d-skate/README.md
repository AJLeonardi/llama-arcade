# 3D Skate

Designed by Mariano. A skateboarding game that tries to look real: a sunny city with glass towers that
reflect the sky, photo asphalt, concrete, and soft shadows.

You start on the roof of a 120 m skyscraper. Push off and drop in on the giant **blue ramp** that runs down
the building, then shoots up a kicker and throws you high over the street: do kickflips and backflips
before you land on the landing ramp. Then skate the city: **10 miles** of street full of rails, ledges,
quarter pipes, mini ramps, fun boxes, stair sets with handrails, and big blue jumps down the middle.
Mile signs tell you how far you've gone.

## How to play

| | Touch (iPad / iPhone) | Keyboard (Mac) |
|---|---|---|
| Push | Left thumb: drag up | ↑ or W |
| Steer | Left thumb: left / right | ← → or A D |
| Brake | Left thumb: drag down | ↓ or S |
| Ollie (jump) | **OLLIE** | Space |
| Kickflip | **KICK FLIP** | F |
| Backflip | **BACK FLIP** | B |
| Spin in the air | Steer while flying | ← → |
| Grind | Ollie onto a rail and land on it, going along it | |
| Look around | Drag on the right side | Drag the mouse |
| Back to the roof | ↺ | R |
| Pause | ⏸ | Esc / P |

Tricks need to be finished before you land, or you fall (and get back up a moment later). Trick, spin and
air points add up; your best score is saved. **Board** picks one of six boards (some push faster, some
jump higher) and **Skater** changes skin, hair, shirt, pants, shoes and helmet.

## For grown-ups

- No ads, no accounts, nothing sent anywhere. Saves (board, skater, best score) stay on the device.
- Falls are cartoon falls: the skater tips over and gets back up.
- It's the heaviest game in the arcade to draw (a real-looking city). If the iPad struggles, tell Claude.
- Debug: add `?z=2000` to the URL to start 2 km down the street.
