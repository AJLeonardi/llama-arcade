# Llama Life

Live a life in a real-looking town (in the style of 3D Skate). You're a person (not a llama). You already
have a **mansion** in the suburb: walk out the front door, get in your car, and go wherever you like.
There's nothing to win and no police. You just live and explore.

- **Llama Latte** ☕: walk up to the counter and get a coffee, a hot chocolate, a muffin or a donut.
- **FreshMart** 🛒: walk down the aisles and take whatever you want (apples, bananas, bread, milk, cookies,
  cereal, carrots, cheese, pasta, watermelon, ice cream, pizza). No paying!
- **Sunny Hill School** 🏫: go to class (8 AM to 3 PM) and learn a fact, or read a book in the library.
  There's a field and a basketball court out the back.
- **Work** 💼: Llama Corp's tower in the city. Do some work (8 AM to 6 PM) and you earn a **new car**:
  Big SUV, Pickup Truck, Sports Car, Limo, Supercar, Monster Truck. After that, golden trophies. New cars
  park in front of your mansion.
- **Your mansion** 🏡: the bedroom (sleep in your bed, change clothes at the wardrobe), the living room
  (watch TV), the kitchen (juice from the fridge, pancakes at the stove), and a pool out the back.
- **City Park** 🌳 with a fountain, and a country road between the suburb and the city.

**Day and night:** a day takes 12 minutes. The windows and street lamps light up at night. You can only
sleep when it's dark (7:30 PM to 6 AM): go to your bed and tap **Sleep**, and you wake up at 7 the next
morning.

**Your bag** 🛍️ holds 12 things. Tap it, then tap something to eat or drink it.

**Map and GPS:** the round map turns with you. Tap it (or press M) for the big map and pick where you want
to go: a blue line shows the way along the roads, a blue beam of light shines over the place, and the top of
the screen says how far it is. Drive (or walk) there; the GPS turns off when you arrive.

## How to play

| | Touch (iPad / iPhone) | Keyboard (Mac) |
|---|---|---|
| Walk / run (on foot) | Left thumb anywhere on the left side; push far to run | W A S D or arrows |
| Drive | Left thumb: up = gas, down = brake / reverse, sideways = steer | W A S D or arrows |
| Look around | Drag on the right side | Drag the mouse, or Q / E |
| Do things (Get in, Sleep, Take…) | The big yellow button | F or Enter |
| Jump (on foot) / Honk (driving) | Blue button | Space (H honks too) |
| Map / bag / pause | Tap the round map / 🛍️ / ⏸ | M / B / Esc |

## For grown-ups

Saves in the browser (day, time, cars earned, bag, how you look). "Start a new life" on the title screen
resets it. Debug: `?hour=21` starts at 9 PM, `?at=work` (or `home`, `coffee`, `market`, `school`, `park`)
starts at that place.
