# I Need Money

A theme park game. You start with only **$5**, and your whole park depends on money. Build rides and roller
coasters with the builder arm on your right hand, guests pay to ride, and you collect the cash to build
bigger and better rides. 30 things to build, from a $5 Tiny Slide to the $1,000,000 Llama King Coaster.

## How to play

- **Walk:** put your left thumb down anywhere on the left side and move it (Mac: WASD or the arrow keys).
- **Look:** drag on the right side (Mac: drag the mouse, or Q/E). **Jump:** the ⬆️ button (Mac: space).
- **🏗️ Build** (Mac: B): pick a ride, look where you want it (green ring = OK, red = something's in the
  way), then tap **✅ Build it!** Your arm shoots a beam and the ride grows out of the ground.
- **Collect money:** walk up to a ride when 💵 is floating by its entrance. Guests also give you tips when you
  walk past them.
- **Next to a ride:** 🎢 **Ride it!** (🚪 Get off to stop), 🔧 **Fix it!** if it broke (stay close while you
  fix it), 💰 **Sell** (half price back; tap twice).
- **The Up-and-Down Tower** can be made **⬆️ BIGGER!** again and again, forever. Each time it gets taller,
  holds more guests and costs more to ride.
- **👷 Hire** (Mac: H): **workers** collect money from every ride for you; **mechanics** run to broken
  rides and fix them. Each one costs more than the last.
- **⏸️** has the 🏠 Arcade button and 🔄 Start over. The park saves by itself.

## For grown-ups

- Saves in the arcade's storage (`llama-arcade/i-need-money/save`): money, rides, helpers.
- No ads, no real money, no network play. Everything is drawn in code.
