# Llama Soccer 3D

You are **Mr. Random**, a llama who plays for the Revolution and just got picked for **Team USA** at the
World Cup. USA starts in **last place** (#16 of 16). Win matches to climb the table, then win the Final at
#1 to become World Champions.

## How to play

- **PLAY** starts a match against the team just above USA in the table. Matches are 5 llamas a side and
  about 2½ minutes long (the clock shows 0' to 90').
- **Run:** left thumb anywhere on the left side of the screen (a joystick appears). On the Mac: WASD or
  the arrow keys.
- **Shoot or pass:** when Mr. Random has the ball, **draw a line** with your other finger (or the mouse).
  When you let go, the ball follows your line. The game slows down while you draw. A **curvy line** tricks
  the keeper, because he guesses from the way the ball is going right now. Space bar = a quick shot
  straight at the corner.
- **Steal the ball:** run into the llama who has it.
- **Winning moves USA up** one place for every goal you win by (up to 3). Losing never moves you down.
- **Coins:** 10 per goal, plus 30 for a win, 15 for a draw, 10 for a loss.

## My Team

Tap a llama, then tap another to swap them: two positions on the field, or a bench player coming on.
Mr. Random can play anywhere but can't go to the bench. **New players** are for sale under the bench and
get better as USA climbs. Stats: ⚡ speed, 🦶 kick, 🧤 saves (only matters for the keeper). Up to 10 llamas
in the squad; 👋 lets a bench player go.

## For grown-ups

- Progress is saved on the device (`llama-arcade/llama-soccer-3d/save`).
- The countries are real; the players are made-up llamas.
- ⏸ pauses; the 🏠 Arcade button is on the home screen and in the pause menu.
