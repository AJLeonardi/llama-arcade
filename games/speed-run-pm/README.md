# Speed Run PM

The night-time sister of Speed Run AM, designed by the kids. You run down one endless straight road made of a
giant 3D keyboard, level after level, and **your speed never stops going up**, all the way to ∞. No doors, no
cape, no restarting, no ads.

## How to play

- **Run** with the joystick (touch the left side of the screen) or W/A/S/D / the arrow keys. **Jump** with
  the JUMP button or Space. Drag the screen (or Q/E) to turn the camera.
- Your **speed** (⚡, top left) starts at 1:
  - Every new keyboard key you run over adds 1 (click-clack!).
  - **Free treadmills** make it go up and up: the small ones (×1, ×2, ×3, ×5) at the start of every level,
    and the big ones (×10, ×20, ×50, ×100) right in front of the speed wall. They're all free. The further
    out, the faster.
  - Your speed is never lost: not when you fall, not between levels, not the next day.
- Each level is a long keyboard road with rails on both sides (you can't fall off), and one thing between each
  long stretch:
  1. **Goo**: one on level 1, two after that. Jump over it. If you're super fast you skim right over it
     ("Too fast for the goo!").
  2. **The speed wall**: it shows the speed you need (10, 30, 100, 330, 1K, 3.3K...). Fast enough and you
     smash through. Too slow? The big treadmills are right there in front of it.
  3. **A jump pad**: it bounces you up high, just for fun.
  4. **The finish line** and the cup. Then you just keep running: the road goes straight on into the next
     level. Levels get a bit longer each time.
- Splat in the goo and you come back just behind it, with all your speed. Nothing ever sends you back to
  the start.
- The levels never run out. Speeds get short names: K (thousand), M (million), B (billion), T (trillion),
  and then up to ∞ if you keep going.

## For grown-ups

- Saves the level and the speed on the device (`llama-arcade/speed-run-pm/save`).
- The pause menu has the title screen; the title screen has 🏠 Arcade. Starting the game again puts you at the
  start of the level you're on.
