# Texture sources

All planet, Sun, Moon, Saturn ring and Milky Way pictures are from Solar System Scope
(https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), CC BY 4.0, which are based on NASA mission images.
They were shrunk and converted to WebP. Terraformed Mars and the Biggest Star are made in code from the
Mars and Sun pictures.

`nebula.webp` (what's left after a supernova) is the Crab Nebula seen by the James Webb Space Telescope,
NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI, T. Temim (Princeton University), from https://esawebb.org/images/weic2326a/
(CC BY 4.0), shrunk to 1024×1024.
